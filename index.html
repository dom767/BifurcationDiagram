About

This is the bifurcation diagram of the logistic map xn+1 = r · xn · (1 − xn). The horizontal axis is the growth parameter r; the vertical axis is the long-term value of x.

As r increases, stable fixed points give way to period doubling and then chaos— the classic cascade discovered by Robert May and analysed by Mitchell Feigenbaum. Dense bands are chaotic attractors; gaps are periodic windows.
